The kingdom runs on Foundry's world time. Advance it however you normally do (SmallTime, the PF2e world clock, or a macro), and the module catches up on whatever time has passed, up to six months at once. Dates follow PF2e's world clock.

Every week

  • Income is paid into the treasury, and food is bought for whatever the farms don't cover.
  • Each filled seat's stat grows a little.
  • A desk matter lands on the quietest desk.

Every month

  • One to three major events from the adventure's table, more as the kingdom grows, placed near the party's level.
  • Trouble for settlements missing what they need (none in the kingdom's first three months).
  • An audience for one seat, and a council reminder in chat.
  • Fame +1. At 10 or more Unrest: Ruin, and a flat check to keep a hex.
  • The ruler's powers come back.

Whenever time moves

  • Finished work is rolled. Results wait a day so players can reroll, then apply.
  • Problems past their deadline apply their failure.
  • Rank-ups that are done move on to their choice.

Desk matters, audiences and the council reminder can each be turned off in the settings.

Everything runs in the GM's game: with no GM logged in, nothing happens until one is. During combat the kingdom waits, then catches up.

A quick way to move time: a script macro with game.time.advance(7 * 86400) jumps a week; use 30 * 86400 for a month.
