What you need

  • Foundry v13 or v14 with the Pathfinder Second Edition system.
  • The official Pathfinder: Kingmaker module, with its adventure imported into this world. Without it, desks, cards and the treasury still work, but nothing on the map does.
  • A party actor with the player characters in it.

Checklist

  1. Import the story text. The first time you load, the module offers to copy the adventure's regions, locations, events, quests, characters and structures into GM-only journals. You can re-import any time from Setup; pages you've edited are left alone.
  2. Run Setup (opens automatically the first time; later from Module Settings or the gear on the Realm tab):
  3. The kingdom: its name and its ruler (one player character; never an NPC). Saving Setup founds the kingdom; its level always follows the party's.
  4. Table options: Tap the Treasury.
  5. The capital: found it here (a settlement already on the map, or a new name).
  6. Founding focus: the two realm stats the party picks to start at rank 1. Locked once saved; "Pick again" takes it back.
  7. Seats: who holds each of the six seats (one player can hold two) (a PC, or a companion actor who suits the seat), an optional companion advisor (each seat lists only the companions who suit it), and the player who supervises a companion's seat. A seat nobody at the table plays (an NPC holder, or a companion or hired advisor) with no supervisor is open to every player; pick a supervisor to give it to one person. An empty seat can take a hired advisor: they roll at −2, have no special ability, and cost 1 BP a week, but the desk works and its stat grows.
  8. Advisors: every companion and adventure NPC who can advise a seat (two built in for each seat, Oleg and Svetlana Leveton, Kesten Garess and Akiros Ismort among them), with the seats they suit. Tick Known once the party has met them; only known advisors appear in the seat lists, so nobody is spoiled. Oleg, Svetlana and Kesten start known. Set each one's attitude as their Influence changes: below friendly they won't use their seat's special ability, below indifferent they stop advising. At the bottom you can add any NPC by name and seat (the world's NPCs are suggested as you type, so Jaethal, Nok-Nok, Jhod Kavken or Bokken are a few keystrokes away); they work like any other advisor.
  9. Tell the players to open the Player's Handbook in the journal sidebar.

The kingdom's numbers

The Kingdom Manager keeps the kingdom's numbers itself: level, Unrest, the four Ruins, Fame, size and food. The PF2e kingdom sheet isn't used, and its Kingdom tab is hidden on the party sheet. There's no charter, heartland, government or skill training to fill in: a kingdom check is d20 + kingdom level + the stat's rank bonus + bonuses − penalties, against the Control DC. See the GM's Numbers page.

Seats and companions

A seat counts as filled when it has a holder, a willing companion advisor, or a hired advisor. A companion actor holding a seat counts as its advisor unless another companion is picked. A companion placed in a seat they don't suit (from an older setup) doesn't count, and Setup marks them so you can pick again. Filled seats grow their realm stat every week; empty ones don't, and their cards can only be handled personally. A companion advisor gives that desk's advisor rolls a status bonus (+1, +2 from level 8, +3 from level 16) and grows the stat a little faster. Every seat has a special ability, which appears under that desk's Start project while a friendly companion advises it.

Joining mid-campaign

If you install the module partway through the adventure, open Setup → Joining mid-campaign? (also on the Realm tab's GM tools). It shows a nudge there when the party is 3rd level or higher and every realm stat is still at rank 0.

  • Realm stats: suggested ranks for the party's level. The founding stats are at the cap, filled seats one below and empty seats two below. Change any of them. Earlier rank-up choices are skipped.
  • Treasury: suggested at about a month of base income.
  • What's already standing: on the Settlements tab, the GM's + on a track raises it a stage for free, with no roll and no Unrest or Fame.

The weekly catch-up growth only closes a gap of a rank or two; it isn't meant to carry a kingdom from rank 0 to mid-campaign.
