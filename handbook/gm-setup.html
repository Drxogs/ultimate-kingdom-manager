What you need

  • Foundry v13 or v14 with the Pathfinder Second Edition system.
  • The official Pathfinder: Kingmaker module, with its adventure imported into this world. Without it, desks, cards and the treasury still work, but nothing on the map does.
  • A party actor with the player characters in it.

Checklist

  1. Build the kingdom. On the party sheet's Kingdom tab, finish the builder: charter, heartland, government, trained skills and ability boosts. Advisor rolls use these kingdom skills. Skip this and everything rolls at an untrained +0.
  2. Import the story text. The first time you load, the module offers to copy the adventure's regions, locations, events, quests, characters and structures into GM-only journals. You can re-import any time from Setup; pages you've edited are left alone. The structure list for building projects is read from these pages.
  3. Run Setup (opens automatically the first time; later from Module Settings or the gear on the Realm tab):
  4. Table options: Tap the Treasury.
  5. Founding training: the four kingdom skills the party trains at founding, on top of the government's two, however many players there are. The same as setting them on the PF2e sheet's Activities tab.
  6. Founding focus: the two realm stats the party picks to start at rank 1. Locked once saved; "Pick again" takes it back.

No invested leaders. This module doesn't use the kingdom sheet's Invested tick boxes: they're hidden, and any that get ticked are unticked. Training comes from the party's founding picks instead, and the status bonus is left free for companion advisors and rank-up rewards. - Seats: who holds each of the eight seats (a PC, or a companion actor who suits the seat), an optional companion advisor (each seat lists only the companions who suit it), and the player who supervises a companion's seat. A seat nobody at the table plays (an NPC holder, or a companion or hired advisor) with no supervisor is open to every player; pick a supervisor to give it to one person. An empty seat can take a hired advisor: they roll at −2, have no special ability, and cost 1 BP a week, but the desk works and its stat grows. - Companion attitudes: set each companion's attitude as their Influence changes in play. Below friendly, they won't use their special ability. Below indifferent, they stop advising, and the desk says so. 4. Tell the players to open the Player's Handbook in the journal sidebar.

Seats and companions

A seat counts as filled when it has a holder, a willing companion advisor, or a hired advisor. A companion actor holding a seat counts as its advisor unless another companion is picked. A companion placed in a seat they don't suit (from an older setup) doesn't count, and Setup marks them so you can pick again. Filled seats grow their realm stat every week; empty ones don't, and their cards can only be handled personally. A companion advisor gives that desk's advisor rolls a status bonus (+1, +2 from level 8, +3 from level 16) and grows the stat a little faster. Every seat but the Ruler's has a special ability, which appears under that desk's Start project while a friendly companion advises it. The Ruler is always a party member or a hired advisor.

Joining mid-campaign

If you install the module partway through the adventure, open Setup → Joining mid-campaign? (also on the Realm tab's GM tools). It shows a nudge there when the party is 3rd level or higher and every realm stat is still at rank 0.

  • Realm stats: suggested ranks for the party's level. The founding stats are at the cap, filled seats one below and empty seats two below. Change any of them. Earlier rank-up choices are skipped.
  • Treasury: suggested at about a month of base income.
  • Kingdom skills: optional. Applies a typical training plan to the PF2e kingdom sheet: four trained skills at founding, then an increase at every odd level from 3rd, raising those four evenly. Leave it unticked if your players have already chosen.
  • Ability boosts: optional. Fills any level 5, 10, 15 or 20 boosts that were never chosen.
  • Buildings already standing: on the Settlements tab, the GM's Place a building button (house with a tick) adds a building for free, with no roll and no Unrest or Fame.

The weekly catch-up growth only closes a gap of a rank or two; it isn't meant to carry a kingdom from rank 0 to mid-campaign.
