What you need

  • Foundry v13 or v14 with the Pathfinder Second Edition system.
  • The Pathfinder: Kingmaker module, with its adventure imported. Without it, the desks, cards and treasury still work, but nothing on the map does.
  • A party actor with the player characters in it.

Checklist

  1. Import the story text. The first time you load, the module offers to copy the adventure's regions, locations, events, quests, characters and structures into GM-only journals, so cards can link to the right pages. You can re-import from Setup at any time; pages you've edited are left alone.
  2. Run Setup. It opens by itself the first time; afterwards use Module Settings or the gear on the Realm tab.
  3. The kingdom: its name and its ruler, who must be a player character.
  4. Your table: whether players may tap the treasury, and whether new map pins are shown to players straight away.
  5. The capital: pick a settlement already on the map, or name a new one. It starts as a village with Huts, a Campfire and a Shrine. If you rename it later, rename its label on the map too.
  6. Founding focus: the two realm stats the kingdom was built around. They start at rank 1.
  7. Seats: who holds each of the six seats. A player can hold more than one. Each seat can also have a companion advisor, and a player to supervise it.
  8. Advisors: the companions and adventure NPCs who can advise each seat. Tick Known once the party has met them; only known advisors are offered for seats, so nobody is spoiled. Oleg, Svetlana and Kesten are known from the start. Set each one's attitude as their Influence changes. You can also add any NPC as an advisor; start typing a name and the world's NPCs are suggested.
  9. Point your players to the Player's Handbook in the journal sidebar.

Seats, advisors and who can act

  • A seat with a holder, a willing companion advisor or a hired advisor is filled. Filled seats grow their stat each week and can delegate cards. An empty seat's cards can only be handled personally.
  • A companion advisor gives that desk's rolls a bonus (+1, then +2 from level 8 and +3 from level 16), grows the stat a little faster, and brings the seat's special project. Below friendly they won't do the special project; below indifferent they stop advising.
  • An empty seat can take a hired advisor instead: −2 on rolls, no special project, 1 BP a week.
  • A player can act for the seats their character holds and the seats they supervise. A seat that no player owns or supervises is open to every player.

The kingdom's numbers

The Kingdom Manager keeps the kingdom's numbers itself: level, Unrest, Fame, Ruin, size and food. PF2e's own kingdom sheet isn't used and its tab is hidden. If this world already had a kingdom on that sheet, its numbers are read in once, the first time you load.

The kingdom's level always matches the party's. There's no charter, heartland or government to choose, and no skill training. To set a number by hand, use the pencil in the window's header.

Joining mid-campaign

If you start using the module partway through the adventure, open Setup → Joining mid-campaign? (also among the Realm tab's GM tools). It suggests realm stat ranks for the party's level and a starting treasury, and you can change either. To add what your settlements already have, use the + on each track on the Settlements tab; it's free.
