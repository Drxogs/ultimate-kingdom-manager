The odds

Before you delegate or handle something personally, the card shows how likely it is to succeed:

Label Chance of at least a success
Very easy 80% or more
Easy 60–79%
Even odds 40–59%
Hard 20–39%
Very hard under 20%

Advisor rolls are kingdom checks: d20 + kingdom level + the stat's rank bonus + bonuses − penalties, against the kingdom's Control DC plus the card's difficulty. The rank bonus is +3 at ranks 1–2, +6 at ranks 3–5, +9 at ranks 6–8 and +11 at ranks 9–10. Bonuses come from advisors, buildings and rewards; penalties from Unrest and Ruin. Personal rolls use your character's skill against the standard DC for the party's level, plus the card's difficulty.

When the roll happens

The check rolls on its own when the work time is up. The result then waits on the card:

  • Not happy? Spend Fame to reroll it (the card has the button). When you handled it personally, you can also reroll your own check from chat the normal PF2e way, with a hero point.
  • The realm's rerolls. Some rewards give the realm rerolls for certain skills. They're listed on the Realm tab under Rerolls, and a card whose skill has one shows a blue "reroll available" tag. When that card's result is waiting, click Reroll on it: the check is rolled again and the new result stands. One reroll per check, and they run out at the date shown.
  • Happy? Click Accept.
  • Do nothing and it applies after one in-game day.

The result, and everything it changed, is posted to chat.

Flat checks

Some results and the monthly Unrest check call for a flat check (a plain d20 against a DC, usually 11). These aren't rolled behind the scenes: chat posts a card with a Roll flat check button, and any player can press it. The roll shows in chat, and the card then says what happened.
