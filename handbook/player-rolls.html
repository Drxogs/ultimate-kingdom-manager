The odds

Before you commit, each card shows how likely it is to go well:

Label Chance of at least a success
Very easy 80% or more
Easy 60–79%
Even odds 40–59%
Hard 20–39%
Very hard under 20%

When an advisor handles a card, they roll d20 + kingdom level + the stat's rank bonus + bonuses − penalties against the kingdom's Control DC, plus the card's difficulty. Bonuses come from advisors, settlements and rewards; penalties from Unrest and Ruin.

When a character handles it personally, they roll their own skill against the usual DC for the party's level, plus the card's difficulty.

When the roll happens

The check rolls by itself when the work is done, and the result waits on the card:

  • Accept it, or leave it: it applies after a day.
  • Spend Fame to roll again.
  • Use a realm reroll if the kingdom has one for that skill (the Realm tab lists them).
  • If you handled it personally, you can reroll your own check from chat with a hero point.

A check can only be rerolled once. The result, and everything it changed, goes to chat.

Flat checks

Some results call for a flat check: a plain d20, usually against 11. It shows up in chat with a button that any player can press.
