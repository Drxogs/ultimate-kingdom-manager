The odds

Before you delegate or handle something personally, the card shows how likely it is to succeed:

Label Chance of at least a success
Very easy 80% or more
Easy 60–79%
Even odds 40–59%
Hard 20–39%
Very hard under 20%

Advisor rolls use the kingdom's skill against the kingdom's Control DC, plus the card's difficulty. Personal rolls use your character's skill against the standard DC for the party's level, plus the card's difficulty.

When the roll happens

The check rolls on its own when the work time is up. The result then waits on the card:

  • Not happy? Reroll from the chat card the normal PF2e way, with a hero point if you have one. When you handled it personally, the chat card is yours to reroll.
  • Happy? Click Accept.
  • Do nothing and it applies after one in-game day.

The result, and everything it changed, is posted to chat.
