The map stays yours

Claims, roads, farmland, work sites and settlements are marked with the Kingmaker module's own hex tools, as usual. The Kingdom Manager reads the map (what's claimed, farmed, worked and settled) and never changes it. Only claimed hexes count toward the kingdom.

GM tasks

When something needs doing by hand, it goes on the GM tasks list on the Realm tab and is whispered to you:

  • A project is done: mark the claim, road, farm, work site or new settlement on the map. Players say where, and any note they left is in the task. To fortify a hex, open it in the Kingmaker hex editor and tick Fortified.
  • An event did something the module can't: a farm destroyed, a leader out of action, a settlement losing a stage (use the − on its track).

Tasks with a place have a jump-to-hex button. Tick them off when they're done.

Map pins

Cards tied to a hex or settlement get a pin on the Stolen Lands map. By default only you can see them. Press the eye on a card to show it to the players: its pin then opens a short page in the Kingdom Notices journal saying what it is, which desk has it, and the deadline. Projects are always shown. If you'd rather every new pin be shown to players, turn that on in Setup.

When a card is resolved, its pin and notice go away. If you prefer, a setting keeps a small marker instead.

The hex panel

Open a hex in the Kingmaker hex panel and a Kingdom section lists the cards active there. Players see only the ones you've shown and projects.

Where events turn up

Events, desk matters and quests prefer zones within about a level of the party, so the kingdom's work tends to be where the party ought to be. Threats avoid fortified hexes.
