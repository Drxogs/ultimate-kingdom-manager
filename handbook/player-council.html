The council

BP is limited, so the kingdom has to choose. Instead of starting a project, any seat can propose it. Proposals wait on the Council tab with their cost and time.

  • Everyone votes. Click again to take your vote back.
  • The ruler decides: Fund pays for it and puts it on its desk; Reject turns it down.
  • Whoever proposed it can withdraw it.

At the start of each month, chat lists what's waiting and how much is in the treasury: a good moment for a short council scene.

Audiences

Once a month a petitioner comes before one of the seats: a widow with a land dispute, a playwright whose play the reeve wants banned, a merchant who wants a monopoly. Press Hear them, read the request, and choose your answer. Every answer shows what it will do, and there's no roll.

Answering adds a point to your seat's stat. Leave a petitioner waiting two weeks and they go home angry: Unrest +1.

Seat ambitions

Each seat has a three-step ambition on the Realm tab, like Builder of Towns for the Viceroy. Each step brings a reward. See Seat Ambitions.
