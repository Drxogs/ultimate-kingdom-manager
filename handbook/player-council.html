The council

BP is limited, so the kingdom has to choose. Any seat can propose a project instead of starting it. Proposals sit on the Council tab with their cost and work time.

  • Everyone votes thumbs up or down. Click again to take your vote back.
  • The ruler decides: Fund pays for it and puts it on its desk; Reject turns it down.
  • Whoever proposed it can withdraw it.

At the start of every month, a chat post lists what's waiting and how much is in the treasury. That's a good time for a short council scene.

Audiences

Once a month, a petitioner comes before one of the seats: a widow with a land dispute, a playwright whose play the reeve wants banned, a merchant asking for a monopoly. Click Hear them, read what they want, and choose your answer. Every answer shows its effects before you pick, and there's no roll.

Answering adds a point to your seat's stat. Leave a petitioner waiting two weeks and they go home angry: +1 Unrest.

Seat ambitions

Each seat has a three-step ambition on the Realm tab, like Builder of Towns for the Viceroy. Each step reached gives a reward and a mention in chat. See Seat Ambitions.
