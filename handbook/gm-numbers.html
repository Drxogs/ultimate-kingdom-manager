For when you want to know why something costs what it costs.

Income

Weekly base income is the PF2e monthly resource roll at its average, split into four: (level + 4) dice of the kingdom's resource die (d4 at territory size up to d12 at dominion), averaged, divided by 4. About 3 BP a week at level 1, 16 at level 10, 39 at level 20. On top of that: work sites (0.5 BP a week each, 1 on a matching resource hex), realm ranks (1 per rank, capped at the Economy rank), trade agreements and settlement perks (a Market +0.5, Bazaar +1, Pavilion +2, Inn +1...). Debt cuts the total by a quarter.

Conversions

1 RP = 1 BP. Lumber, stone, ore and food = 2 BP each. Luxuries = 4 BP. A "resource die" from an event is one die's average, spread over the next four weeks.

Kingdom checks

The module keeps the kingdom's numbers itself (the PF2e kingdom sheet isn't used). A kingdom check is d20 + kingdom level + rank bonus + bonuses − penalties:

Stat rank 0 1–2 3–5 6–8 9–10
Rank bonus +0 +3 +6 +9 +11
  • Bonuses: a companion advisor (status, +1 to +3 by level), the settlement's tracks (item: +1 to +3, each skill from one track only), rank-up and ambition rewards (status). The best of each type counts. Crime checks in a settlement whose top Homes stage is Tenements take −1 (circumstance).
  • Unrest (status penalty to every check): −1 at 1–4, −2 at 5–9, −3 at 10–14, −4 at 15 or more.
  • Ruin (item penalty): Corruption hurts Politics, Intrigue, Statecraft, Arts and Folklore; Crime hurts Trade, Industry and Boating; Decay hurts Agriculture, Wilderness, Exploration, Magic, Scholarship and Engineering; Strife hurts Warfare and Defense. Each Ruin's threshold starts at 10; reaching it sets that Ruin back to 0, adds −1 to its skills, and raises the threshold by 2.
  • Degrees follow PF2e: 10 over or under shifts a step, a natural 20 or 1 one more.

DCs

  • Control DC: the level-based DC for the kingdom's level, +1 at province size (10+ hexes), +2 state (25+), +3 country (50+), +4 dominion (100+).

  • Advisor rolls: Control DC + the card's modifier.

  • Personal rolls: the standard DC for the party's level + the card's modifier.
  • Projects: Control DC.

Realm stats

20 points per rank. Cap: half the party level, rounded up. Points: +3 / +2 / 0 / −1 by result, −2 for a missed problem, +1 for an answered audience, about +0.5 a week from a filled seat (+0.75 with a suited companion), multiplied while two or more ranks behind the cap. A well-run seat gains about a rank every six months.

Settlements

  • Size from Homes: village (Huts), town (Tenements, level 3), city (Neighborhoods, level 9), metropolis (Boroughs, level 15). Other tracks go at most one stage past Homes.
  • Food need by size: 1, 2, 4, 6, less the settlement's Food stage and a Granary Hall's −2 everywhere.
  • Trouble: 1 in 3 per missing track each month, 1 in 4 for Tenements, none for 90 days after the capital is founded.

Costs

  • Rank-up: two weeks of base income, 14 days.
  • Claim, road, farmland: half a week of base income; work site: one week. 7 days each.
  • Settlement tracks: starter 2 BP / 3 days, +1 8 BP / 7 days, +2 25 BP / 14 days, +3 60 BP / 21 days, pinnacle 120 BP / 28 days, less any discounts.
  • Tap the Treasury: up to the Core Rulebook's "currency per additional PC" for the level, costing a month of base income at the full amount.

Pacing

Built for a campaign of four to six in-game years: 40 major events plus 3 story events, 96 desk matters in three level versions, 24 audiences, 16 trouble cards, twelve settlement tracks and three-step ambitions for every seat.
