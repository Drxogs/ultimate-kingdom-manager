For when you want to know why something costs what it costs.

Income

Weekly base income is the PF2e monthly resource roll at its average, split into four: (level + 4) dice of the kingdom's resource die (d4 at territory size up to d12 at dominion), averaged, divided by 4. About 3 BP a week at level 1, 16 at level 10, 39 at level 20. On top of that: work sites (0.5 BP a week each, 1 on a matching resource hex), realm ranks (1 per rank, capped at the Economy rank) and trade agreements. Debt cuts the total by a quarter.

Conversions

1 RP = 1 BP. Lumber, stone, ore and food = 2 BP each. Luxuries = 4 BP. A "resource die" from an event is one die's average, spread over the next four weeks.

DCs

  • Advisor rolls: Control DC + the card's modifier.
  • Personal rolls: the standard DC for the party's level + the card's modifier.
  • Projects: Control DC.

Realm stats

20 points per rank. Cap: half the party level, rounded up. Points: +3 / +2 / 0 / −1 by result, −2 for a missed problem, +1 for an answered audience, about +0.5 a week from a filled seat (+0.75 with a suited companion), multiplied while two or more ranks behind the cap. A well-run seat gains about a rank every six months.

Costs

  • Rank-up: two weeks of base income, 14 days.
  • Claim, road, farmland: half a week of base income; work site: one week. 7 days each.
  • Structures: the adventure's RP and materials, converted to BP. 14 days plus one per level above 4.
  • Tap the Treasury: up to the Core Rulebook's "currency per additional PC" for the level, costing a month of base income at the full amount.

Pacing

Built for a campaign of four to six in-game years: 43 major events, 96 desk matters in three level versions, 24 audiences, three-step ambitions for every seat.
