For when you want to know why something costs what it costs.

Income

Weekly base income is the PF2e monthly resource roll at its average, spread over four weeks: about 3 BP a week at level 1, 16 at level 10, 39 at level 20. On top of that come work sites (0.5 BP a week each, 1 on a matching commodity), realm ranks (1 per rank, but no stat gives more than Economy's rank), trade agreements and settlement perks (a Market +0.5, a Bazaar +1, a Pavilion +2, an Inn +1, and so on). Debt cuts the total by a quarter.

Food: each settlement needs food by its size (1, 2, 4, 6), less its Food track. Each claimed farm hex covers 1. The treasury pays half a BP a week for each point short; if it can't, Unrest +1.

Kingdom checks

d20 + kingdom level + rank bonus + bonuses − penalties, against the Control DC plus the card's difficulty.

Stat rank 0 1–2 3–5 6–8 9–10
Rank bonus +0 +3 +6 +9 +11
  • Bonuses: a companion advisor (status, +1 to +3 by level), the settlement's tracks (item, +1 to +3), rank-up and ambition rewards (status). Only the best of each type counts.
  • Unrest (status penalty): −1 at 1–4, −2 at 5–9, −3 at 10–14, −4 at 15+.
  • Ruin (item penalty): Corruption hurts Politics, Intrigue, Statecraft, Arts and Folklore; Crime hurts Trade, Industry and Boating; Decay hurts Agriculture, Wilderness, Exploration, Magic, Scholarship and Engineering; Strife hurts Warfare and Defense. A Ruin's threshold starts at 10; reaching it adds −1 to its skills, resets the Ruin, and raises the threshold by 2.
  • Crime checks in a settlement whose best homes are Tenements take a −1.
  • Degrees of success follow PF2e.

DCs

  • Control DC: the level-based DC for the kingdom's level, +1 at 10+ hexes, +2 at 25+, +3 at 50+, +4 at 100+.
  • Advisor rolls and projects: Control DC plus the card's modifier.
  • Personal rolls: the standard DC for the party's level plus the card's modifier.

Realm stats

20 points per rank, capped at half the party's level rounded up. Points: +3, +2, 0, −1 by result; −2 for a missed problem; +1 for an answered audience; about +0.5 a week from a filled seat (+0.75 with a suited companion), more while a stat is two or more ranks behind. A well-run seat gains about a rank every six months.

Costs

  • Rank-up: two weeks of base income, 14 days.
  • Claim, road, farmland: half a week of base income; work site: a week. 7 days each.
  • Settlement stages: 2 BP and 3 days for a starter, 8/7 for +1, 25/14 for +2, 60/21 for +3, 120/28 for a pinnacle, less any discounts.
  • Tap the Treasury: up to the Core Rulebook's "currency per additional PC" for the level, costing a month of base income at the full amount.

Settlements

  • Size from Homes: village, then town (level 3), city (level 9) and metropolis (level 15). Other tracks can be at most one stage ahead of Homes.
  • Trouble: 1 in 3 per missing track each month, 1 in 4 for Tenements, none in the first 90 days.

Pacing

Built for a campaign of four to six in-game years: 40 major events and 3 story events, 96 desk matters in three versions by level, 24 audiences, 16 trouble cards, twelve settlement tracks, and three-step ambitions for every seat.
