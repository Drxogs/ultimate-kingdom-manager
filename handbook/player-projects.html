Projects are work you choose to start. Click Start project on a desk, pick what and where, and you'll see the cost, the work time and the advisor's odds.

  • Start pays the cost now and puts the project on the desk.
  • Propose to council puts it on the Council tab for a vote instead.

Once it's on the desk, a project works like any other card: delegate it or handle it personally, the work timer runs, and the check rolls.

Result What happens
Critical success Done, and half the cost comes back
Success Done
Failure Not done; half the cost comes back. Try again
Critical failure Not done, the money is gone, +1 Unrest

When a project is done, the GM marks it on the map.

The kinds

See Project Reference for the full list. In short:

  • Claim a Hex (Warden): an unclaimed hex next to the realm.
  • Build a Road, Establish Farmland (Viceroy): in a claimed hex.
  • Establish a Work Site (Treasurer): a lumber camp, quarry or mine. On a hex with that resource it pays double.
  • Build a Structure (Viceroy): a building in one of your settlements, up to the kingdom's level.
  • Companion abilities: when a companion advises a seat, their special ability appears here too. Only the advisor can do it, and it has a cooldown. See Companion Abilities.

Distance and danger

Work in zones well above the party's level costs more and takes longer. Work in safe, settled land is cheaper.
