Projects are work you choose to start, once the realm has a capital. Click Start project on a desk and pick what to do. You'll see the cost, the work time and the advisor's odds. For work on a hex (claims, roads, farmland, work sites, fortifications) you don't pick the hex in the window: tell the GM where, as you would at the table. You can jot a short note ("east of Monkeyton") and it goes into the GM's task.

  • Start pays the cost now and puts the project on the desk.
  • Propose to council puts it on the Council tab for a vote instead.

Once it's on the desk, a project works like any other card: delegate it or handle it personally, the work timer runs, and the check rolls.

Result What happens
Critical success Done, and half the cost comes back
Success Done
Failure Not done; half the cost comes back. Try again
Critical failure Not done, the money is gone, +1 Unrest

When a project is done, the GM marks it on the map at the hex you chose.

The kinds

See Project Reference for the full list. In short:

  • Claim a Hex (Warden): an unclaimed hex next to the realm.
  • Build a Road, Establish Farmland (Viceroy): in a claimed hex.
  • Establish a Work Site (Treasurer): a lumber camp, quarry or mine. On a hex with that resource it pays double.
  • Build a Structure (Viceroy): a building in one of your settlements, up to the kingdom's level and with room on its urban grid. Also from Build here on the Settlements tab. See Settlements and Buildings.
  • Found a Settlement (Viceroy): a new village in a claimed hex.
  • Fortify a Hex (General): a watchtower and earthworks. Threats tend to turn up elsewhere.
  • Muster the Troops (General): once a month, a bonus to Warfare and Defense for 30 days and a little less Crime.
  • Seat abilities: when a companion advises a seat, the seat's special ability appears here too. Only the advisor can do it, and it has a cooldown. See Companion Abilities.

Danger

If the party heads into land far above its level, an advisor may speak up in chat.

On the map

Projects and anything the GM has revealed show as pins on the Stolen Lands map, with a short notice in the Kingdom Notices journal. Open a hex in the Kingmaker hex panel to see what's going on there.
