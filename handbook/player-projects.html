Projects are work you choose to start. Press Start project on a desk and pick one: you'll see its cost, how long it takes, and your advisor's odds. For work out on the map (claims, roads, farms, work sites, fortifications), tell the GM which hex, as you would at the table. You can leave a short note ("east of the capital") for them.

  • Start pays now and puts the project on the desk.
  • Propose to council puts it to a vote instead.

From there a project works like any card: delegate it or handle it personally.

Result What happens
Critical success Done, and half the cost comes back
Success Done
Failure Not done; half the cost comes back
Critical failure Not done, the money's gone, and Unrest +1

When it's done, the GM marks it on the map.

What you can start

  • Viceroy: Claim a Hex, Build a Road, Establish Farmland, Found a Settlement.
  • Treasurer: Establish a Work Site (a lumber camp, quarry or mine). It pays double on a hex with the matching commodity.
  • General: Fortify a Hex (threats tend to go elsewhere) and Muster the Troops (once a month: a bonus to Warfare and Defense for 30 days).
  • Settlements: each track is built from its row on the Settlements tab. See Settlements and Tracks.
  • Seat abilities: a companion advising a seat brings a special project of their own. See Companion Abilities.

The Project Reference page lists them all.

On the map

Projects, and anything the GM chooses to show, appear as pins on the Stolen Lands map with a short page in the Kingdom Notices journal. Open a hex's panel to see what's going on there.
