Your party doesn't just explore the Stolen Lands, it rules them. Ultimate Kingdom Manager runs the kingdom the way a strategy game does: things happen on the calendar, every leader has a desk full of matters to deal with, advisors take days to get work done, and the kingdom grows as you do.

Opening the Kingdom Manager

Click the chess rook button in the token tools on the left of the screen, or the rook button at the top of the party sheet. The window has five tabs:

  • Realm: the treasury, Unrest, Fame, Ruin, the six realm stats, the crown (the ruler's powers) and every seat's ambition.
  • Desks: one desk for each leadership seat. Desks that belong to you are highlighted.
  • Settlements: a picture of each settlement, its tracks (how far its homes, temples, markets and walls have grown) and what it still needs.
  • Council: projects someone has proposed, waiting for votes and funding.
  • Chronicle: a running log of what's happened in the kingdom.

The first step

A new kingdom's first job is to found its capital: name it and tell the GM where it stands. Until then the desks stay quiet. See Settlements and Tracks.

Your seat

Player characters hold the six leadership seats: Emissary, Counselor, General, Magister, Treasurer and Viceroy (one player can hold two). One of you is also the ruler, a title rather than a seat. Seats the party doesn't hold go to companion advisors, and each of those has a player who supervises it, so every desk has someone at the table looking after it.

Everything that lands on a desk is that seat's job. You can act on any desk you hold or supervise.

The kingdom is always your level

The kingdom's level is always the party's level. There's no kingdom XP to track. When you level up, so does the realm.
