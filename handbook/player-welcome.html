Your party doesn't just explore the Stolen Lands, it rules them. The kingdom runs on the calendar: things happen while you travel, every leader has a desk of matters to deal with, advisors take days to get work done, and the realm grows as you do.

Opening the Kingdom Manager

Click the rook in the token tools on the left of the screen (or on the party sheet). The window has five tabs:

  • Realm: the treasury, Unrest, Fame and Ruin, the six realm stats, the ruler's powers, what the land holds, and each seat's ambition.
  • Desks: one desk per leadership seat, with the cards waiting on it.
  • Settlements: each settlement, how far it has grown, and what it still needs.
  • Council: projects someone has proposed, waiting for votes and funding.
  • Chronicle: what has happened in the kingdom, newest first.

Your seat

The kingdom has six leadership seats: Emissary, Counselor, General, Magister, Treasurer and Viceroy. Player characters hold them, and a player can hold more than one. One player character is also the ruler, a title on top of their seat.

Seats nobody at the table holds can be run by companions you've met on the road. Each of those can have a player who looks after it, so every desk has someone watching it.

You can act on any desk you hold or look after.

Getting started

The first thing a new kingdom needs is a capital. Tell the GM where it stands and what it's called; until it's founded, the desks stay quiet.

Your level is the kingdom's level

The kingdom is always the party's level. There's no kingdom XP to track: when you level up, so does the realm.
