In the header

  • Pencil (Edit realm stats): set the kingdom's numbers by hand: BP, Unrest, Fame, each Ruin's value, threshold and penalty, trade agreements, the realm stats' ranks and points, and the seat ambitions. Use it for rulings, results the module can't apply, mistakes, or testing.

On cards

  • Roll now: skip the rest of the work time.
  • Record outcome: set the result yourself, for a card the party played out at the table or to overrule a roll.
  • Dismiss: remove a card with no effect.
  • Show to players (eye): show or hide the card's pin on the map.

On the GM tab

Only you see this tab. It has three sections under the GM tasks list.

  • Setting up: Setup, Edit realm stats (the same window as the pencil), Joining mid-campaign? and Import (or Re-import) the story text.
  • Drawing events: Draw events now, Draw audience and Draw desk matter, to fill a quiet month or to test. Pick an event puts any event from the monthly deck on its desk: choose it from the list beside the button.
  • Story events: pick the chapter the party is in, and that chapter's story events are listed (the troll sightings, Grigori the rabble-rouser, the bloom outbreaks and so on). Put it on the desk when the story gets there. Below the list are the chapter's kingdom effects that you apply by hand.

Finish now on a rank-up in progress is on the Realm tab. On the desks, ongoing story cards that end when the story says so (Troll Sightings, the bloom siege) have an It's over button for you. Story cards also carry a GM-only note, and Read it in the adventure opens the adventure's own section on that event when the Kingmaker adventure is imported.

On the Settlements tab

Raise or lower tracks, draw trouble, and manage settlements. See Settlements (GM).

Approvals

If Tap the Treasury needs your approval, you'll be asked whenever a player tries it.

From the console

game.modules.get("ultimate-kingdom-manager").api has open(), setup(), importStory(), handbook(), realm() (the kingdom's data) and tick() (run the clock now).
