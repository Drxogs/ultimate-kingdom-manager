In the header

  • Pencil: set the kingdom's numbers by hand: BP, Unrest, Fame, and each Ruin's value, threshold and penalty. For results the module can't apply, house rulings or mistakes. Nothing else happens when you save.

On cards

  • Roll now (d20): skip the rest of the work time.
  • Record outcome (gavel): set the result yourself, for cards the party played out at the table, or to override.
  • Dismiss (×): remove a card with no effect.
  • Show to players (eye): reveal the card's pin and notice on the map; click again to hide.

On the Realm tab

  • Setup, Re-import story text.
  • Draw events now, Draw audience, Draw desk matter: draw immediately, for testing or to fill a quiet month.
  • Story events: put one of the adventure's story-driven events (the Chapter 5 outbreaks) on its desk when the story reaches it.
  • Finish now (on a rank-up in progress).
  • GM tasks list.

On the Settlements tab

  • − and + on a track's row: lower or raise it one stage for free, with no roll, Unrest or Fame.
  • Lightning bolt: draw one of that settlement's trouble cards now, ignoring the odds and the easy start.
  • Crown, waves, bin: make it the capital, mark it waterside, or remove it from the realm.

Approvals

  • With Tap the Treasury set to "GM approval", you get a yes/no prompt whenever a player tries it.

Things you can do from the console

game.modules.get("ultimate-kingdom-manager").api (or "ultimate-kingdom-manager-next" for the test build) has open(), setup(), importStory(), handbook(), realm() (read the kingdom's data) and tick() (run the clock now).
