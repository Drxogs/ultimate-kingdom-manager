Advisor: the person who works a seat's cards when you delegate. A player character or companion who holds the seat, or a companion advisor assigned to it. Works on one thing at a time.

Ambition: a seat's three-step goal track. Each step gives a reward.

Anarchy: Unrest 20 or more. Every kingdom check is one step worse.

Attitude: how a companion feels about the party. Below friendly they won't use their special ability; below indifferent they stop advising.

Audience: a petitioner's request, answered by choosing a response. No roll.

Basic need: Homes, Food and Faith: every settlement needs at least their starters (Huts, a Campfire, a Shrine).

BP (build points): the kingdom's money. Only buys kingdom things.

Capital: the realm's first settlement, founded before anything else.

Card: anything on a desk: problems, opportunities, desk matters, audiences, projects and trouble.

Catch-up: a realm stat well behind its cap grows faster each week.

Commodity: lumber, stone, ore or luxuries on a hex. A work site on the matching commodity earns double.

Control DC: the kingdom's base DC, set by its level and size. Advisor rolls are against it.

Council: where proposed projects get votes and the ruler decides what's funded.

Critical success / success / failure / critical failure: PF2e's four degrees of success. Every card lists what each one does.

Deadline: when a card runs out. Missed problems count as failures; missed opportunities just go.

Debt: gold drawn from the treasury that hasn't been paid back.

Delegate: hand a card to the seat's advisor.

Desk: a seat's inbox in the Kingdom Manager.

Desk matter: a small weekly card, written in early, middle and late versions so it fits the kingdom's level.

Difficulty label: Very easy to Very hard, the chance of at least a success.

Fame: the kingdom's reputation, up to 3, +1 each month. Spend 1 to reroll a waiting result, or all of it to stop a Ruin penalty or anarchy.

Favour: a one-use reward some cards give the ruler, kept under The crown on the Realm tab until it's spent.

Food need: how much food a settlement needs, by its size, less what its Food track provides. Farms cover the realm's total; the treasury buys the rest, and if it can't, Unrest rises.

Fortified hex: a hex the General has fortified. Threats tend to turn up elsewhere.

Founding focus: two realm stats the party picks at setup that start at rank 1.

GM task: something the GM needs to do by hand, usually marking a change on the map.

Handle personally: send a player character to deal with a card using their own skill. A character from another desk is borrowed, which ties up both desks until the work is done.

Hired advisor: someone paid to run an empty seat. Rolls at −2, has no special ability, costs 1 BP a week.

Kingdom Notices: the journal of things the GM has shown players on the map.

Ongoing: a card that comes back every month until the right result ends it.

Pinnacle: a track's great work, kingdom-wide. Only a metropolis can build one, and there's only one of each in the realm.

Project: work started on purpose: claims, roads, farms, work sites, settlement tracks, seat abilities.

Rank / rank cap: a realm stat's level, up to half the party's level rounded up. Ranks add +3, +6, +9 or +11 to their skills' checks.

Rank-up: a two-week project that raises a realm stat and ends with a choice.

Realm stat: one of six bars, one per seat, filled by rolls on its skills.

Routine growth: the small weekly gain every filled seat gives its stat.

Royal powers: the ruler's Decree (reroll a waiting result, keep the better), Court (Unrest −1) and Charge (a project finishes a week sooner). Each once a month.

Ruin: Corruption, Crime, Decay and Strife. When one fills up, it leaves a lasting −1 on its skills.

Ruler: the player character the GM names king or queen. A title, not a seat; it brings the royal powers (Decree, Court, Charge) and favours.

Seat: one of the six leadership roles: Emissary, Counselor, General, Magister, Treasurer, Viceroy.

Seat ability: each seat's special project, used by the companion advising it. Advisor only, 30-day cooldown.

Settlement size: village, town, city or metropolis, set by the Homes track (Tenements need kingdom level 3, Neighborhoods 9, Boroughs 15).

Starter: a track's first stage. It opens the track and meets a basic need, but gives no skill bonus.

Status bonus: a PF2e bonus type. Only the biggest status bonus on a check counts.

Supervisor: the player who looks after a seat run by a companion.

Tap the treasury: the one way to take gold out of the kingdom. It leaves a debt.

Tenements: Homes +1. Turns a village into a town, but while they're its best homes, crime is likelier there and crime checks are harder.

Track: one line of growth in a settlement (Homes, Faith, Commerce...): a starter, then +1, +2, +3.

Trade agreement: adds a little BP every week.

Trouble card: a problem a settlement draws when it's missing something it needs, or living in Tenements.

Unrest: how unhappy the realm is. It gives −1 to −4 on every kingdom check; at 10+ it adds Ruin each month and can cost a hex; at 20 the realm falls into anarchy.

Windfall: the bit of extra BP every critical success brings.

Work site: a lumber camp, quarry or mine. Earns BP each week, double on a hex with the matching commodity.

Work time: how long the advisor or character is busy before the check rolls.
