If your GM has turned it on, you can work your desk while the GM is offline: delegate, handle personally, propose, vote, answer audiences. Your requests are saved and applied when the GM next logs in. Depending on the GM's setting, they apply straight away or the GM approves each one.

Rolls only happen when the GM is online, so nothing resolves without them.

If between-session play is off, you can still open the Kingdom Manager and read your desk, but you can't act until the GM is back.
