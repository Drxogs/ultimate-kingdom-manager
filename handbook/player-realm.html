The kingdom has six realm stats, one for each seat. Each is fed by two or three of the kingdom's skills.

The ruler

The ruler isn't a seat: the GM names one player character king or queen, and they also hold whatever seat they sit in. The ruler has three powers, each once a month (Realm tab, The crown):

  • Royal Decree: a waiting result is rolled again, and the better of the two stands. Use it from the card.
  • Royal Court: hold court in the capital. Unrest −1.
  • Royal Charge: a project underway finishes a week sooner. Use it from the project's card.

Some cards also give the ruler a favour: a one-use reward they keep until they spend it, listed under The crown.

Filling the bar

  • Every roll on a stat's skills adds points: critical success +3, success +2, failure 0, critical failure −1.
  • A problem left past its deadline costs −2.
  • Answering an audience adds +1 to that seat's stat.
  • Every seat with someone in it adds a little each week on its own, about half a point. A companion advisor adds a bit more. An empty seat adds nothing.
  • A stat that's fallen well behind what your level allows grows faster until it catches up.

Ranking up

Each rank costs 20 points. A stat can't rank higher than half the party's level, rounded up (rank 1 at level 1, rank 10 at level 20). A stat can store one extra rank's worth of points, so it can rank up right away when you level.

When the bar is full, Begin rank-up on the Realm tab. It costs about two weeks of income in BP and keeps that seat's advisor busy for 14 days. When it's done, the seat's player chooses one of three rewards. Each shows its effects before you choose. To look ahead, the eye button next to a stat's rank shows the three choices waiting at its next rank, any time, without spending anything. See Rank-Up Choices.

What a rank gives

  • A bonus to the stat's two skills: +1 at ranks 1–2, +2 at ranks 3–5, +3 at rank 6 and up.
  • +1 BP a week per rank, but no stat can add more than the Economy rank. Grow Economy early.

Bonuses that don't stack

Some rewards give a status bonus. In PF2e, bonuses of the same type don't add up; only the biggest counts. If a check already has a status bonus (a companion advisor, or an earlier reward), a reward that gives one might add little or nothing. Rewards never go to waste: if a rank-up or ambition reward's status bonus wouldn't add anything, you get two rerolls on the same checks instead, and the choice screen says so.

Founding focus

When the kingdom is set up, the party picks two realm stats the kingdom was built around. Both start at rank 1.

Fame and Unrest

Fame (or Infamy) is the realm's reputation, up to 3 points. The realm gains 1 at the start of every month, and more from great results, famous buildings and some rank-ups.

  • Spend 1 Fame to reroll a result that's waiting on a card (the star button). The new result stands, and a check can only be rerolled once.
  • Spend all of it to hold off disaster. When a Ruin would reach its limit and add a penalty, or Unrest would reach 20, the GM is asked whether the party spends all its Fame to stop it.

Unrest is how unhappy the realm is. It gives a penalty to every kingdom check: −1 from 1 Unrest, −2 from 5, −3 from 10, −4 from 15.

  • 10 or more: at the start of each month the realm gains 1d10 Ruin, spread over the four kinds, and chat asks for a DC 11 flat check, which any player rolls with the button on the chat card. On a failure the realm loses a hex (the GM unclaims one the party picks).
  • 20 or more: anarchy. Every kingdom check is one step worse until Unrest drops below 20.

Critical successes

Every critical success on a card brings a small windfall on top of its result: about a day's income in BP (at least 1).
