The kingdom has eight realm stats, one for each seat. Each is fed by two of the kingdom's skills.

Seat Realm stat Fed by
Ruler Loyalty Politics, Intrigue
Counselor Culture Arts, Folklore
General Military Warfare, Defense
Emissary Relations Statecraft, Boating
Magister Arcane Magic, Scholarship
Treasurer Economy Trade, Industry
Viceroy Community Agriculture, Engineering
Warden Stability Wilderness, Exploration

Filling the bar

  • Every roll on a stat's skills adds points: critical success +3, success +2, failure 0, critical failure −1.
  • A problem left past its deadline costs −2.
  • Answering an audience adds +1 to that seat's stat.
  • Every seat with someone in it adds a little each week on its own, about half a point. A companion advisor in a role that suits them adds a bit more. An empty seat adds nothing.
  • A stat that's fallen well behind what your level allows grows faster until it catches up.

Ranking up

Each rank costs 20 points. A stat can't rank higher than half the party's level, rounded up (rank 1 at level 1, rank 10 at level 20). A stat can store one extra rank's worth of points, so it can rank up right away when you level.

When the bar is full, Begin rank-up on the Realm tab. It costs about two weeks of income in BP and keeps that seat's advisor busy for 14 days. When it's done, the seat's player chooses one of three rewards. Each shows its effects before you choose. To look ahead, the eye button next to a stat's rank shows the three choices waiting at its next rank, any time, without spending anything. See Rank-Up Choices.

What a rank gives

  • A bonus to the stat's two skills: +1 at ranks 1–2, +2 at ranks 3–5, +3 at rank 6 and up.
  • +1 BP a week per rank, but no stat can add more than the Economy rank. Grow Economy early.

Bonuses that don't stack

Some rewards give a status bonus. In PF2e, bonuses of the same type don't add up; only the biggest counts. If a check already has a status bonus (a companion advisor in a seat they suit, or an earlier reward), a rank-up choice that gives one might add little or nothing. The choice screen tells you when that's the case. Ambition rewards never go to waste: if their status bonus wouldn't add anything, you get two rerolls on the same checks instead.

Founding focus

When the kingdom is set up, the party picks two realm stats the kingdom was built around. Both start at rank 1.
