Advisor rolls look low at the start. A stat at rank 0 adds only the kingdom level. The two founding stats start at rank 1 (+3), and the rest catch up as their desks earn points. Joining partway through the adventure? Use Setup → Joining mid-campaign?

Delegate doesn't show on a card. The seat has no holder or advisor, or the advisor is busy (with another card, a project or a rank-up), or the desk is tied up by a borrowed character. Handle it personally, or fill the seat in Setup.

A seat's special project is missing. No companion advises that seat, the companion is less than friendly, or the project is resting.

A player can't act on a desk. Players can only act for seats their character holds or that they supervise. A seat whose character no player owns is open to everyone. Check holders and supervisors in Setup.

Nothing happens when time passes. The kingdom runs in the GM's game: make sure a GM is logged in, and that Setup has been saved.

No hexes to claim. The realm has no claimed hexes yet, or none with unclaimed neighbours. Mark the starting claims with the Kingmaker hex tools.

Farms or camps aren't counted. Only claimed hexes count. Check the hex is claimed in the hex editor; the Realm tab's The land shows what the kingdom sees.

A settlement drawn on the map isn't on the Settlements tab. It joins when you save the map, if its hex is claimed and it has a name. A name that matches a settlement already in the realm is treated as the same place.

No trouble cards. They're a chance, not a certainty, and the first three months are quiet. The lightning bolt on a settlement draws one now; if it says everything is already out, resolve one of that settlement's trouble cards first.

Tapped gold didn't arrive. If the character sheet can't take coins directly, chat asks you to add the gold by hand.
