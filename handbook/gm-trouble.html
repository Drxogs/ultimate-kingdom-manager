Advisor rolls look low at the start. A stat at rank 0 adds nothing but the kingdom level. The two founding-focus stats start at rank 1 (+3); the rest catch up as their desks earn points. Joining mid-campaign? Use Setup → Joining mid-campaign?

Delegate doesn't show on a card. Nobody holds that seat and it has no companion advisor, or the advisor is already busy (on another card, a project or a rank-up), or the desk is tied up because a character was lent to or from it. Handle it personally, or fill the seat in Setup.

A seat's special ability is missing. No companion advises the seat, the companion's attitude is below friendly, or the ability is on cooldown.

A player's button does nothing. Players can only act on desks they hold or supervise. Check the seat's holder and supervisor in Setup.

Nothing happens when time passes. The clock runs on the GM's client. Make sure a GM is logged in and Setup has been saved (saving Setup founds the kingdom).

"No GM is online" for players. Desks only act while a GM is logged in. Players can still read them.

No hexes to claim. The realm has no claimed hexes yet, or no unclaimed neighbors. Mark the starting claims with the Kingmaker hex tools.

Tap the Treasury gold didn't show up. If the character sheet doesn't support adding coins directly, the chat post asks you to add the gold by hand.

A settlement drawn on the map isn't on the Settlements tab. It joins when you save the map, if it's in a claimed hex and has a name. A settlement whose name matches one already in the realm (or that sits on a settlement's remembered hex) is treated as the same place.

No Build button on a track. Only the player who holds or supervises that track's seat sees it (Faith and Performance are the Counselor's, and so on). A seat whose character no player owns is open to everyone.

No trouble cards. They're a chance, not a certainty, and none come in a kingdom's first three months. The Settlements tab shows how long the easy start has left. The lightning bolt draws one now.

The lightning bolt says every card is already out. Every trouble card that settlement can draw is already on a desk. Resolve one and it can come again.

Dates look like real-world dates. Dates follow PF2e's world clock. If PF2e's clock isn't available, Foundry's own calendar is used, which has Earth month names.

After coming from v0.22, the numbers look wrong. Unrest, Fame and Ruin are read from the PF2e kingdom sheet once, the first time the GM loads. If the sheet had been reset by then, set them by hand with the pencil in the manager's header; they won't be read again.
