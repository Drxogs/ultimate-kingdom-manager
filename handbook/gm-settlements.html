The capital comes first

Until a capital exists, no events, desk matters or audiences are drawn and no projects start. You found it in Setup → The capital (or from the banner on the manager's tabs): pick a settlement already drawn on the Kingmaker map, or name a new one and you get a GM task to claim the hex and draw it. The capital starts with Huts, a Campfire and a Shrine.

The map and the realm

The module keeps its own list of settlements and their tracks. The Kingmaker map only shows where they are.

  • A settlement on the map links to the realm's one by name. Name the map feature exactly as the players named it and the jump-to-hex button appears.
  • Settlements you draw on the map after the capital exists join the realm as soon as you save the map, with Homes to match their size (a town gets Tenements) and nothing else.
  • Renaming a settlement in Setup doesn't rename it on the map, and the map's old label won't come back as a second settlement.
  • The panorama uses the terrain of the settlement's hex; settlements not on the map show plains.
  • Events that happen in a settlement pick from this list too; a settlement drawn on the map also gets a pin.

Tracks replace buildings

Settlements no longer have an urban grid. Each has twelve tracks (see the players' Settlements and Tracks page). Worlds from before tracks are converted the first time the GM loads: each building raises the track it belongs to (a shrine is the Faith starter, a temple Faith +2), kept within what the settlement's size allows. The old building list is kept in the data, untouched.

Events that ask whether the kingdom has a certain building (a dump, a sewer system, a cemetery) read it from the tracks.

GM tools on the Settlements tab

  • − and + on a track's row lower or raise it one stage for free, with no roll, no Unrest and no Fame. Use them for things standing before the module, rubble from an event, or a mistake.
  • Crown: make another settlement the capital.
  • Waves: mark a settlement as waterside (the Great Bridge needs it).
  • Bin: remove a settlement from the realm. The map isn't changed.

Perks you run by hand

A few pinnacles do things the module doesn't automate: the Arena's games, the Counting House's cheaper loans, Embassy Row's extra audience answer, the Shadow Network's warning, the Watchtowers' bonus and the Knightly Order and University extras. Their text says what to do.
