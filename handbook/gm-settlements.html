The capital comes first

Until a capital exists, no events, desk matters or audiences are drawn and no projects start. You found it in Setup → The capital (or from the banner on the manager's tabs): pick a settlement already drawn on the Kingmaker map, or name a new one and you get a GM task to claim the hex and draw it. The capital comes with a free Founders' Hall (a home that also cuts the capital's food need by 1).

The map and the realm

The module keeps its own list of settlements and their buildings. The Kingmaker map only shows where they are.

  • A settlement on the map links to the realm's one by name. Name the map feature exactly as the players named it and the jump-to-hex button appears.
  • Settlements you draw on the map after the capital exists join the realm by themselves, empty.
  • The Settlements tab is the one list. It's copied to the PF2e kingdom sheet (name, size, level, overcrowded, food reduction) so the sheet's Consumption is right. A settlement typed on the PF2e sheet by hand is moved into the tab (or dropped if the tab already has it), and you get a whispered note, so nothing is counted twice.
  • Events that happen in a settlement pick from this list too; a settlement drawn on the map also gets a pin.

Buildings

The list of buildings, their costs, lots, traits and bonuses is read from the Structures journal of the imported story content, so it matches the book. Bonuses to tabletop activities are turned into the kingdom skill that activity uses. Two have no skill and are left to you: the guildhall's bonus and the watchtower's (+1 to checks on cards about that settlement, which the module does apply).

GM tools on the Settlements tab

  • × on a lot removes that building: for rubble from an event, or a mistake.
  • Crown: make another settlement the capital.
  • Waves: mark a settlement as waterside (mills only cut food by the water).
  • Bin: remove a settlement from the realm. The map isn't changed.

Overcrowding

Each overcrowded settlement adds 1 Unrest at the start of every month.
