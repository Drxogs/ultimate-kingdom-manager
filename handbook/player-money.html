Build points

The kingdom's money is BP. The treasury earns BP every week from the realm's size and level, work sites, realm ranks, trade agreements and the markets and inns in your settlements. It also pays for any food your farms don't cover. Hover the BP in the window's header to see where it comes from.

BP only buys kingdom things: projects, rank-ups and settlement growth.

The kingdom's money isn't yours

BP never turns into gold for your character, with one exception.

Tap the treasury

The Treasurer's desk has a Tap the treasury button that takes gold out for one character. The GM may need to approve it.

  • How much you can take depends on the kingdom's level (30 gp at level 3, 500 gp at level 10).
  • It costs about a month of income in BP.
  • Every draw adds Unrest +1 and Corruption +1, and chat says who took the money.
  • The realm now owes it back. Until it's repaid, income drops by a quarter, Economy can't rank up, and every further draw is worse and might cause a public scandal.

Anyone can Repay the debt from their own gold, from the Realm tab.
