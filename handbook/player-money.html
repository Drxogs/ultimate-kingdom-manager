Build points

The kingdom's money is BP (build points). The treasury earns BP every week from the realm's size and level, work sites, realm ranks and trade agreements, and pays for food the farms don't cover. BP only buys kingdom things: projects, rank-ups, buildings.

Kingdom money is not your money

BP never turns into gold for your character, with one exception.

Tap the treasury

The Treasurer's desk has a Tap the treasury button. It pulls gold out for one character. Your GM may need to approve it.

  • You can take up to a set amount for the kingdom's level (30 gp at level 3, 500 gp at level 10).
  • It costs about a month of income in BP.
  • Every draw adds +1 Unrest and +1 Corruption, and a public chat post names who took the money.
  • The realm now owes the gold back. Until it's repaid, weekly income drops by a quarter, Economy can't rank up, and every further draw is two degrees worse and might cause a public scandal.

Anyone can Repay the debt from their own gold with the button on the Realm tab.
